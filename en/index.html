Ricardo Rapozo

Memory is a space in dispute. That's why I chose to work with narratives. My path began in television, in the late nineties, and has spanned almost three decades of audiovisual work across advertising, documentaries, television and personal projects. Along the way I passed through the editing room, direction, actor coaching, motion design and post-production. I've never seen these areas as isolated disciplines. They all answer the same question: how does an idea take shape, rhythm and meaning? Today I develop projects from concept to final finish. Research, script, direction, editing, animation and finishing are all part of the same creative process. Movement is the consequence of a well-built structure. Images are the molds of our memories.

EXPERTISE

No tool creates a good idea. They only give it form. My work combines direction, narrative, design, composition and post-production to develop visual solutions that stay coherent from concept to final delivery.

Research

Every narrative begins before the camera. Investigating the context, understanding the problem and finding the right language are part of the creative process. Research isn't just about gathering references; it guides the decisions made throughout the entire project.

Direction

Direction means defining how a story will be perceived. Rhythm, framing, cinematography, editing, movement and silence work together to guide the eye and build meaning. Nothing happens by accident.

Process

A good process creates space for good decisions. Organization, standardization and planning reduce friction, avoid rework and keep the energy where it really matters: in the creation.

Post-Production

Post-production is where all the layers come together. Editing, motion design, composition and finishing transform isolated materials into a single experience. Technique exists to serve the narrative, never the other way around.
